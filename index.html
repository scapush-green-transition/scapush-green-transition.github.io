System Guarantee

Why "Zero-Mortality"?

Traditional African smallholder farming suffers up to 40% livestock mortality during winter seasons due to unmonitored thermal swings. Scapush Smart Farming utilizes digester thermal heat pumps powered by BESS to lock nursery temperatures at optimal biological thresholds.

Result: 99.8% Survival Rate in Piglets & Brooders
